01 / About

A bit about me

I studied Computer Science at Sathyabama Institute of Science & Technology and started my career as a backend engineer at Walmart Global Tech, building services and data pipelines that had to hold up under peak-sale traffic. In 2025 I joined Meesho's AI Services team, where I own enterprise Voice AI deployments end to end: onboarding customers, defining the KPIs we track, and deciding what to fix or build next.

Alongside work, I'm completing BITSoM's Product Management with Gen & Agentic AI program, where I built TripGenie, a GenAI trip-planning prototype, as my capstone.

Why product?

Most of my best work started as a customer problem, not a ticket. At Walmart, the fix that mattered wasn't faster support for order edits but a self-service flow that removed those tickets entirely. At Meesho, the biggest win came from noticing that latency, not accuracy, was driving users away. I want to spend my career on that part of the job: finding the real problem and deciding what's worth building.
